# Product Goals and Boundaries

**Source:** PRD v2 DOCX Sections 3.1–3.4 and 4.4.

MVP goal: deliver one evidence-grounded conceptual recovery loop with bounded multi-concept screening, open learning choice, a complete Distributive Property package, concept validation, and a separate SPL Final Assessment.

Success is functional and evidence-bounded. It does not claim population diagnostic accuracy, causal learning gains, general SPL mastery, long-term retention, or broad scale performance.

Excluded: broad curriculum, unrestricted adaptive diagnosis, dynamic curriculum generation, probabilistic mastery estimation, longitudinal assessment, and general-purpose chatbot tutoring. Open decisions OI-01–OI-03 and pending operational specifications remain explicit in [decisions](../decisions.md).
