# Problem and Users

**Source:** PRD v2 DOCX Sections 2.1–2.6.

Students can struggle on a current mathematics topic because prerequisite concepts may be unclear, but an incorrect answer does not prove a conceptual blocker. Correct answers also do not prove conceptual understanding. Early research is exploratory and perceptual; it does not establish prevalence, causality, diagnostic accuracy, or learning effectiveness.

Initial users are high-school students using self-directed support when a mathematics concept or application is difficult. Segment definitions remain provisional and require research validation. ReThink must preserve alternative explanations and communicate insufficient evidence instead of forcing a blocker conclusion.
