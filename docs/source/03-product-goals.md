03. Product Goals & Success Metrics

3.1 Product Goals & Intended Outcomes

ReThink aims to support evidence-grounded mathematics conceptual recovery by helping students
understand  relevant  learning  difficulties, strengthen targeted conceptual understanding, and
evaluate their ability to apply mathematical concepts independently.

The product is guided by four outcome-oriented goals that define the results ReThink intends to
enable for students within its supported learning coverage.

G1 — Provide Actionable, Evidence-Grounded Recovery Direction

ReThink aims to provide diagnostic outcomes that translate observable mathematical evidence into
appropriately qualified learning directions. When evidence supports a conceptual blocker, the
product should help students recognize the relevant concept requiring attention. When evidence is
insufficient or does not support a blocker, ReThink should communicate the assessment outcome
and its limitations without making unsupported conclusions.

Intended Outcome: Students  receive understandable, evidence-grounded  information about
potential conceptual difficulties and appropriate available next steps.

G2 — Support Targeted Conceptual Recovery

ReThink aims to support students in strengthening their understanding of relevant mathematical
concepts through focused learning interventions that preserve active reasoning and encourage
independent application.

Intended Outcome: Students have opportunities to develop stronger conceptual understanding and
improve  their  ability to apply the targeted mathematical concept without  relying  solely on
instructional guidance.

G3 — Provide Evidence-Based Understanding Evaluation

ReThink aims to evaluate demonstrated conceptual understanding beyond the completion of
instructional activities by examining students' ability to apply the targeted concept independently
and recognize its underlying mathematical structure across different problem representations.

Intended Outcome: Students receive appropriately qualified evidence of  their demonstrated
understanding within the supported assessment scope, including indications of understanding that
may require further reinforcement.

G4 — Enable an Understandable Self-Directed Recovery Experience

3.2 MVP Success Definition

ReThink's MVP success  is evaluated through a tiered framework that distinguishes minimum
functional product success from empirical validation of the user experience.

The MVP  is designed to demonstrate one complete, evidence-grounded conceptual recovery
pathway within  its  explicitly supported mathematical scope: Sistem Persamaan Linear as the
selectable topic and Distributive Property as the primary supported conceptual blocker.

3.2.1 Minimum Functional MVP Success

Minimum functional MVP success requires the supported conceptual recovery system to operate
correctly across its intended diagnostic, instructional, and validation processes.

Three success conditions define this requirement:

SC1 — Complete Supported Recovery Path

ReThink must support one complete conceptual recovery pathway, beginning with supported topic
selection  and  diagnostic  assessment,  followed  by  targeted guided  learning, independent
understanding validation, and blocker-level learning results.

The pathway must function end-to-end for  its supported learning scenario without implying
comprehensive mathematics curriculum coverage or recovery of every prerequisite concept.

3.3 Success Dimensions & Metrics

ReThink evaluates product success through four distinct dimensions aligned with its product goals
and tiered MVP success definition.

Each dimension uses primary metric groups supported by additional indicators. These measures
provide evidence of functional performance, decision integrity, user experience, and learning
outcomes without combining them into a single product success score.

3.3.1 Functional Completeness

Functional completeness evaluates whether ReThink correctly executes its supported conceptual
recovery pathway and handles the relevant diagnostic and validation scenarios.

Primary Metric: Supported Scenario Pass Rate

The proportion of executed required functional scenarios that satisfy their defined expected
outcomes.

Supporting Indicators:

    ●​ End-to-end recovery pathway completion in functional testing.
    ●​ Supported diagnostic and validation outcome routing coverage.
    ●​ Number of required scenarios executed, passed, failed, or not yet tested.

Functional success reporting must distinguish successful execution from incomplete test coverage.
Passing all executed scenarios does not establish complete functional verification when required
scenarios remain untested.

remain consistent with mathematical evidence, supported concept coverage, and established

3.3.5 Measurement & Reporting Principles

ReThink's success measurements must follow these principles:

     1.​ Evidence-Specific  Interpretation: Each metric must be interpreted according to  its
      evidence source, assessment coverage, and limitations.
     2.​ Separation of Evaluation Dimensions: Functional correctness, decision integrity, user
       experience, and learning outcomes must be evaluated and reported distinctly.
     3.​ Transparent Coverage: Functional and assessment results must indicate the scope of
       scenarios, concepts, or participants evaluated.
     4.​ Visibility of Critical Failures: Unsupported diagnostic conclusions and invalid understanding
       claims must not be concealed by aggregate success rates.
     5.​ No Unsupported Numerical Targets: Performance thresholds must be justified through
       functional requirements, methodological considerations, or appropriate evaluation evidence.

Reporting must distinguish between functionally verified and not fully verified product behavior, user
experience  findings and unevaluated  usability, demonstrated assessment performance, and
preliminary evidence of learning improvement. Each status must reflect the completeness and
limitations of its supporting evidence.

3.4 Success Boundaries & Non-Goals

ReThink's success claims must remain proportional to  its supported product capabilities, the
mathematical concepts assessed, and the evidence available from product and learning evaluation.

The following boundaries distinguish what the MVP can demonstrate, what remains subject to
empirical validation, and which broader outcomes are not required for MVP success.

3.4.1 Evidence & Claim Boundaries

Diagnostic outcomes  represent evidence-grounded  conclusions  within  ReThink's supported
assessment scope. A Confirmed blocker does not  establish the  definitive cause of every
mathematical difficulty experienced by a student, while Rejected or Uncertain outcomes do not
establish the absence of learning difficulties.

Knowledge validation outcomes represent demonstrated performance within the concepts and
assessment tasks evaluated. An Understood status indicates that the applicable blocker-level
validation conditions have been met; it does not establish comprehensive topic mastery, long-term
retention, or understanding across all mathematical contexts.

Functional verification, user experience validation, and learning effectiveness evaluation provide
different forms of evidence. Success in one dimension must not be presented as proof of success in
another.

Improving targeted conceptual understanding remains an intended product outcome. However,
claims that ReThink has produced learning improvement require an appropriate evaluation design
and supporting evidence. Neither functional completion nor successful assessment performance
alone establishes causal learning effectiveness.

3.4.2 MVP Outcome Boundaries

The ReThink MVP demonstrates a targeted conceptual recovery experience within a deliberately
limited mathematical scope.

Sistem Persamaan Linear is the selectable topic, and Distributive Property is the primary supported
conceptual  blocker.  The MVP  focuses  on  one  complete  recovery  pathway  connecting
evidence-based diagnosis, guided conceptual learning, and blocker-level knowledge validation.

Learning effectiveness within the supported conceptual scope is not a non-goal. It remains an

Future product development may expand concept coverage, diagnostic granularity, adaptive
recovery capabilities, and longitudinal learning evaluation. Such expansions must be supported by

Together, these boundaries ensure that ReThink's MVP can be evaluated against its supported
