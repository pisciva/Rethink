Product Acceptance & PRD Governance

10.1 Product Acceptance Principles

Product acceptance determines whether the implemented ReThink MVP satisfies the applicable
approved product requirements and success conditions.

Acceptance decisions must be supported by relevant verification evidence rather than inferred from
document approval, implementation completion, or demonstration alone.

 Acceptance         Required Interpretation
 Principle

 Requirements       The implemented MVP must be assessed against the applicable
 Compliance         approved functional requirements and their verification criteria.

 Functional Decision  The relevant end-to-end scenarios must demonstrate appropriate
 Integrity              diagnostic conclusions, learning transitions, and validation outcomes.

 Evidence-Based     Acceptance claims must be supported by documented verification
 Acceptance           results and explicitly identified limitations.

 Dependency         Outstanding   decisions  and   specifications   affecting  required
 Resolution          acceptance scenarios must be resolved before those scenarios are
                      considered fully verified.

 Bounded   Product  Functional acceptance must not be presented as independent proof
 Claims                of   learning   effectiveness,  long-term   retention,  or  broader
                      mathematical mastery.

The  applicable success  conditions, requirement  verification bases, and  verification scenario
coverage remain governed by Sections 03 and 05.

This section does not introduce new numerical acceptance thresholds, assessment methods, or
functional requirements.

10.2 PRD Closure & Change Governance

The Overall PRD serves as the controlled product baseline for subsequent product design, technical
implementation, and verification activities.

Once the full document receives final approval, its scope boundaries, requirements, and approved
architectural principles become authoritative references for the MVP.
