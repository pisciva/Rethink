1.1.2 Product Positioning

ReThink is an AI-assisted mathematics conceptual recovery platform that helps students identify
evidence-supported conceptual blockers underlying difficulties with specific mathematics topics,
engage  in targeted guided  learning, and  validate  their understanding through independent
application and transfer.

Unlike general mathematics learning platforms that primarily organize instruction around broad topic
coverage, ReThink focuses on identifying and recovering specific conceptual gaps that may
interfere with a student's learning.

ReThink integrates evidence-based diagnosis, targeted guided learning, and knowledge validation
into a closed-loop recovery process. The platform prioritizes the relevance and depth of supported
conceptual recovery over comprehensive curriculum coverage.

ReThink does not assume that every mathematical difficulty originates from a conceptual blocker,
nor does it claim to diagnose all possible prerequisite gaps. Its diagnostic and recovery capabilities

1.2 Product Vision

    ●​  Vision Statement

To make mathematical learning difficulties easier to understand and overcome by helping students
identify and address gaps in their understanding of foundational mathematical concepts.

    ●​  Vision Context

ReThink envisions a mathematics learning experience where students are better equipped to
recognize the conceptual gaps that may contribute to their learning difficulties and address them
through focused conceptual recovery. Rather than relying solely on repeated explanations or
procedural assistance, students should have opportunities to rebuild relevant understanding and
develop the ability to apply mathematical concepts independently.

This vision guides ReThink's long-term evolution toward broader conceptual coverage and more
targeted, evidence-based recovery capabilities without shifting its focus toward becoming a general
mathematics learning platform.

1.3 Product Description

ReThink provides a self-directed mathematics learning experience focused on identifying and
recovering specific conceptual gaps that may contribute to difficulties with a selected mathematics
topic.

Students begin by exploring a mathematics catalog, selecting a supported topic, and initiating a
targeted diagnostic assessment. ReThink evaluates evidence from student responses to identify
potential conceptual blockers associated with the selected topic. AI assists in interpreting and
synthesizing diagnostic evidence within the boundaries of curated mathematical knowledge,
mathematical verification, and controlled diagnostic decisions.

When a conceptual blocker is supported by sufficient diagnostic evidence, ReThink recommends a
targeted recovery intervention. The intervention combines visual conceptual explanations, worked
examples, and partially guided practice to help students rebuild relevant understanding while
remaining actively engaged in mathematical reasoning.

Following guided learning, ReThink evaluates understanding through independent problem-solving
and transfer tasks. The results provide evidence of the student's ability to apply the targeted

1.5 Product Differentiation

ReThink differentiates  itself through  its focused approach to conceptual recovery, connecting
evidence-based diagnosis, targeted guided learning, and independent understanding validation
within a unified learning experience. Rather than prioritizing comprehensive curriculum coverage or
general-purpose problem-solving assistance, ReThink concentrates on identifying and addressing
specific conceptual blockers that may contribute to students' mathematical difficulties.

A defining characteristic of ReThink  is the relationship between  its diagnostic, learning, and
validation processes. Diagnostic evidence informs the selection of relevant conceptual recovery
interventions, while subsequent independent application and transfer tasks provide evidence of
students' understanding after instructional support is reduced. This creates a closed-loop recovery
process  in which assessment informs learning and learning outcomes are evaluated through
independent application and transfer tasks within the supported assessment scope.

ReThink supports this approach through AI-assisted evidence interpretation combined with curated
mathematical knowledge and  controlled  decision mechanisms.  Its  differentiation  lies  in the
integration and focus of these capabilities rather than the novelty of any individual technique. This
represents the intended product approach and does not imply demonstrated superiority over
existing mathematics learning systems.

1.6 Core Product Principles

ReThink is guided by six core product principles that shape product decisions, preserve the integrity
of its conceptual recovery approach, and provide a consistent foundation for future development.

1. Evidence Before Inference: Diagnostic conclusions and learning states must be grounded in
observable student evidence rather than unsupported assumptions. ReThink recognizes uncertainty
as a valid outcome and avoids inferring conceptual gaps or understanding beyond what the available
evidence supports.

2. Recovery Over Content Coverage: ReThink prioritizes the relevance, depth, and completeness
of supported conceptual recovery over the breadth of mathematics content offered. Expanding
topic coverage  should  not compromise  the  quality  of  diagnosis,  learning  intervention, or
understanding validation.

3. Active Reasoning Over Answer Delivery: ReThink's learning assistance should support students

mathematical knowledge, supported diagnostic coverage, mathematical verification, and controlled
decision safeguards. AI must not independently establish unsupported conceptual blockers or
learning states.

6. Scalable Structure, Controlled Coverage: ReThink is designed to support progressive expansion
across mathematics topics, prerequisite relationships, and conceptual recovery capabilities. The
platform must distinguish between concepts represented in its knowledge structure and concepts
with explicitly supported diagnostic assessments, curated learning interventions, and knowledge
validation capabilities. Architectural extensibility must not be treated as evidence of operational
capability.
